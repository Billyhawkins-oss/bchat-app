01

Welcome to B CHAT

B CHAT is a messaging app built by Billy Hawkins. It lets you chat with family and friends, share photos, send voice notes, make calls, and much more.

02

Sign Up & Log In

Start signup with your real email and password, then create your display name, username, and optional profile photo. Next time, just log in with your username and password.

03

Starting a Chat

Tap the plus button at the top to see all registered users. Tap on a person's name to start a conversation with them. You can search your chats using the search bar.

04

Sending Messages

Type your message and tap the send button. You can use text formatting: wrap words in asterisks for bold, underscores for italic, and tildes for strikethrough. Links are automatically clickable.

05

Photos, Emojis & Voice Notes

Tap the camera icon to send photos. Tap the smiley face to open the emoji picker with 7 categories. Hold the microphone button to record a voice note — release to send, tap X to cancel.

06

Message Actions

Long-press any message to reply, forward, star, react, pin the chat, or delete. Double-tap a message to quickly react with an emoji. Your sent messages show blue read receipts.

07

Voice & Video Calls

In a chat, tap the phone icon for a voice call or the camera icon for a video call. Your real microphone and camera are used. You can mute, use speaker, flip the camera, and end the call. Call duration is logged in the chat.

08

Profile & Gallery

Tap your profile picture in the sidebar to open the photo gallery. Upload photos from your device, set any as your profile picture, or delete them. Tap another user's avatar in a chat to see their profile card.

09

Status Updates

Go to the Status tab to post text or photo updates. Your status is visible to all users and automatically disappears after 24 hours — just like stories. Tap on someone's status to view it with a progress bar.

10

Ads & Announcements

The Ads tab lets you post announcements with a title, description, and photo that everyone can see. Great for family announcements, selling items, or sharing events. You can delete your own ads.

11

AI Assistant

The AI tab gives you a built-in smart assistant. When you're online it now gives real, conversational AI answers to anything — no setup or API key needed. Offline it still helps with math, jokes, facts, riddles, quotes, tips and translations, and can generate images — just say "create an image of" followed by what you want.

12

Settings & Personalization

Tap the gear icon next to your name to open Settings. Add a bio about yourself, pick a custom accent color to personalize the entire app, and choose from built-in wallpaper presets for your chat backgrounds.

13

Shared Media & Disappearing Messages

Tap the media icon in a chat header to view all shared photos in that conversation. Long-press a message and tap Disappearing Messages to auto-delete messages after 5 minutes, 1 hour, 24 hours, or 7 days.

14

Extra Features

Use the sun icon to toggle dark and light mode. Tap the star icon to view all your starred messages. Use the search icon inside a chat to find specific messages. The browser tab shows your unread message count. Change chat backgrounds using the palette icon.

15

Password Security & Privacy

Your account is protected. On the sign up and log in screens, tap the eye icon to show or hide your password, and watch the strength meter as you type to create a strong one. Your password is encrypted before it's saved, so no one — not even the app owner — can read it.

16

Online Status & Last Seen

See who's around. A green dot appears on the avatar of anyone currently online — in your chat list and when you start a new chat. Inside a chat, the header shows whether the person is online, or when they were last seen.

17

Offline Mode & Alerts

B CHAT always tells you when you're offline. A red banner appears at the top, and you'll get a pop-up if you try to send a message without internet. The AI assistant keeps working offline with jokes, facts, riddles and more — it just lets you know it's in offline mode.

18

Choose Your Guide Voice

Prefer a different narrator? Use the 🎙 Voice selector at the top of this guide to switch between two different voices for the spoken tour. Your choice is remembered next time.

19

Chat Like a Pro (Ticks, Swipe & More)

Your sent messages show ticks: grey ✓✓ means delivered, blue ✓✓ means read. Swipe a message to the right to reply to it instantly. When you scroll up in a long chat, tap the down-arrow button to jump back to the latest message. Messages are grouped by day with "Today" and "Yesterday" labels.

20

Your 4-Digit Code & Multiple Devices

Each account gets a unique 4-digit code shown next to your name — share it so family can add you. To start a chat, tap + and enter their code. You can also use B CHAT on any phone or computer — just log in with the same username and password, and all your chats appear.
